Adoption Agreement / Kasunduan sa Pag-aampon:
- Adoption is a lifetime commitment.
- The adopter agrees to provide proper food, shelter, medical care, and humane treatment for the adopted animal for the rest of its life.
- The adopter must not abandon, sell, trade, or give the animal to laboratories or to individuals who may use the animal for fighting, breeding for profit, or harmful purposes.
- If the animal becomes lost, injured, or if the adopter can no longer care for it, the adopter should immediately notify the shelter or organization and, when possible, return the animal.
- The adopter agrees to provide veterinary care as needed and follow recommended vaccinations, preventives, and treatments.
- The organization may request proof of veterinary visits.
- If the animal has not yet been sterilized before adoption and proof of sterilization is requested within a certain period, the adopter agrees to have the procedure done and provide documentation.
- The adopter agrees to periodic home checks or photo updates at a reasonable frequency to help ensure the welfare of the animal.
- The organization should give prior notice before visits when possible.
- After adoption, the adopter accepts responsibility for any bites or damage caused by the animal and agrees to follow local animal control laws and ordinances.
- The shelter or organization reserves the right to reclaim the animal, after due process, if the adopter violates the agreement or if the animal’s welfare is at risk.
